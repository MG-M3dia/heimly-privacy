What’s coming next

Heimly is built by one person. This page shows what is planned — in the order it is likely to arrive.

Next up

  • Share recipes directly. Send a recipe from a browser or a chat to Heimly via "Share", instead of copying and pasting the text.
  • Feedback. A form on this site — without an account and without a sender, so that bugs and wishes do not only arrive through store reviews.

Later

  • Organising recipes. Categories or tags. Only once there are enough recipes to see what people actually search for — deciding earlier means guessing.
  • Converting servings. The quantities are already stored in a structured form for this. What goes onto the shopping list is converted too.
  • Ingredients per step in cooking mode. Under each step, the ingredients that are up right now, to tick off — without opening the overview.
  • Weekly plan. Put recipes on the days of the week and turn them into one shopping list, with identical ingredients merged. The merging is the real core, not the calendar.
  • A photo of the shelf. For "what is actually hiding at the back", a picture is often more use than a list.
  • Securing your access. Today everything lives on the device without an account. If you want, you should be able to secure your access in future, so that changing devices costs you nothing — voluntarily, and not as a hurdle at first launch.
