Shopping list, pantry and recipes

Shopping and cooking, together as a household.

Heimly sorts your shopping list the way you walk through the store, knows what is in the pantry, and turns a pasted recipe into ingredients and steps. No account, no email.

Get it on Google PlayFree, no ads

  • Shopping

    Items sorted by aisle, with quantity and unit recognised as you type. A list can be shared with a link.

  • Pantry

    What is in the freezer, the fridge and the cupboard, visible to everyone in the household — with a reminder before something expires.

  • Recipes

    Paste text from a chat or a blog and Heimly splits it into ingredients and steps. Cooking mode with timer, convert servings, ingredients onto the list.

  • No account

    No email, no password. Whatever you do not explicitly share never leaves your device.

What Heimly looks like

Stays on your device

Lists, pantry and recipes live on your phone. Only what you explicitly share with your household goes to the server — and you can stop sharing at any time.
