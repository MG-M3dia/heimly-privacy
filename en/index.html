Heimly

Shopping list, pantry and recipes for your household — without an account.

  • Shopping — items sorted by aisle, with quantity and unit recognised as you type. A list can be shared with a link.
  • Pantry — what is in the freezer, the fridge and the cupboard, visible to everyone in the household.
  • Recipes — paste text from a chat or a blog and Heimly splits it into ingredients and steps. From there the ingredients go onto the shopping list.
  • No sign-up — no email, no password. Whatever you do not explicitly share never leaves your device.
